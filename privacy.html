Privacy Policy – Matematik Post

Last updated: 30 October 2026

Matematik Post is a personal tool run by the owner of the Matematikte Özgürleş accounts. It is not offered to the public, and only the owner signs in to it.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission, it uploads the owner's own math videos and images to the owner's own Instagram, Facebook, YouTube and TikTok accounts, and reads basic account information (such as the channel or account name) to check that the connection works. From YouTube it uses only the permissions to upload videos and to read the owner's channel details.

What it stores

The sign-in keys are kept only on the owner's own computer. Nothing is stored on a server, and no information about other people is collected.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared with or transferred to anyone. Data obtained through Google APIs is used only to post to the owner's own channel, in line with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions (Google) or in each platform's settings, and by deleting the keys from the computer.
